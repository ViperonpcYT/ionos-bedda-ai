About

Built for the build.

OnlyBikes (brand site: onlybikes.shop) is a Canadian shop for riders who treat their Surron, Talaria, or E-Ride like a project—not a stock bike sitting in the garage. If you searched "only bikes," use OnlyBikes Canada to find this store.

We stock the parts people actually burn through and the upgrades that change how the bike looks and rides: Ultra Bee brake kits, titanium hardware, supermoto wheels, Baja lights, off-road tire sets, and curated build bundles.

Every listing includes fitment notes so you know what you are buying. If you are stacking mods for the season, OnlyBikes Rewards gives you points back on every order.

Questions before you pull the trigger? Reach out—we would rather answer than send you the wrong part.

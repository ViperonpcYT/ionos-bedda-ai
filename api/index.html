Why Customers Trust Bedda for Sensitive Skin

Simple, honest skincare made for real life. No harsh chemicals. No synthetic fragrances. Just ingredients that work with your skin — not against it.

Quality Canadian Tallow in Every Soap

Our tallow soaps use responsibly sourced Canadian beef tallow — rich in oleic and stearic acids that support skin barrier function. For our balms and creams, we upgrade to grass-fed tallow for maximum nutrient density. All batches are small, fresh, and free from synthetic additives.

Hand-Poured in Ontario

Every bar is crafted using the traditional hot-process method, then cured 4–6 weeks for a gentle, long-lasting lather. We list every ingredient — and why it's there. No "fragrance" loopholes. No greenwashing.

Made for Reactive, Dry, or Eczema-Prone Skin

Unscented options avoid essential oil triggers. Formulas prioritize barrier-supporting lipids over harsh cleansers. Many customers with psoriasis, rosacea, or sensitive skin find relief switching to Bedda's gentle, minimalist approach.

Support Local, Choose Sustainable

Tallow is a nutrient-dense byproduct of Canadian beef production — reducing waste while nourishing skin. Zero palm oil. Minimal plastic packaging. When you choose Bedda, you support a Canadian family business and responsible sourcing.

Questions We Hear Often

Is tallow soap safe for eczema or very sensitive skin?

Many customers with eczema, psoriasis, or reactive skin find Bedda's unscented tallow soap gentle and soothing. Tallow contains fatty acids (oleic, stearic, palmitic) that resemble skin's natural lipids, helping support barrier repair. We avoid sulfates, synthetic fragrances, and common irritants. As with any new product, patch test first and consult your dermatologist if you have specific concerns.

What's the difference between your soaps and balms?

Great question! Our soaps use quality Canadian tallow + olive/coconut oils for a gentle, moisturizing cleanse. Our balms and creams use grass-fed tallow as the base for deeper nourishment and targeted repair. Both have unscented options available. Think: soap for daily cleansing, balm for extra-dry patches or overnight care.

Do you ship across Canada? Is local pickup available?

Yes! We ship nationwide. Live in the Greater Toronto Area, withfree local pickup at checkout. We also occasionally partner with local wellness studios for pop-ups — follow us on Instagram or subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated.

How do you source your tallow?

Transparency matters. Our soap tallow comes from trusted Canadian suppliers who prioritize humane handling. For our balms and creams, we specifically source grass-fed tallow for its higher concentration of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). All tallow is a byproduct of the food industry — we believe in using the whole animal respectfully and reducing waste.

Skincare shouldn't be complicated. If you're looking for a gentle, effective option for sensitive, dry, or eczema-prone skin — made locally with honest ingredients — we'd be honoured to be part of your routine.

Kit questions

Why buy a kit instead of individual parts?

Kits bundle parts that riders commonly install together and discount the total versus separate listings. You still must verify fitment for your exact bike, year, and setup before checkout.

Can I return a kit if something does not fit?

No. All sales are final — including kits, misfit, and wrong fitment. See our No-Return Policy and Fitment page before you order.

Can I mix and match parts in a kit?

Kits are fixed pairings for the best value. To buy à la carte, browse all parts and add only what you need.

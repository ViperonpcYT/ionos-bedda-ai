Fitment Guide

E-moto platforms share a lot of parts—but not all of them. A few minutes of checking before checkout saves a wasted install day. If it does not fit, we do not take returns — ever.

All sales final. OnlyBikes is not responsible if a part does not fit your bike. Dimensions and compatibility notes on product pages are guides only. You must verify everything before you order.

1. Know your bike

Start with the exact model and generation: Surron Light Bee X or S, Talaria Sting / XXX, E-Ride Pro, etc. Year and controller revision can matter for electrical parts.

2. Read the fitment line on every product

Each listing includes a Dimensions & details toggle on the shop page. Use it — then compare to your stock part. We are not liable if you skip this step.

3. Match physical dimensions

  • Brake pads: compare pad outline and pin location to your caliper.
  • Bolt kits: confirm thread pitch and length against factory hardware.
  • Wraps and decals: measure your battery box or fork leg and expect trim-to-fit on universal kits.
  • Lights and accessories: check voltage, connector type, and mounting hole spacing.

4. When in doubt, ask first

Email support@onlybikes.shop with your bike model, year, and a photo of the area you are upgrading. We will tell you honestly if a part is a safe bet or a gamble — but the final decision and risk remain yours.

5. Installation safety

Brakes, pegs, and electrical mods affect rider safety. Use proper tools, torque specs, and thread locker where appropriate. If you are not comfortable with the work, have a qualified shop install it.

Installed or modified parts cannot be returned. See our No-Return Policy.

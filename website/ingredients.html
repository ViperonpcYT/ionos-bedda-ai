Why We Use Non–Grass-Fed Tallow for Our Soaps

At Bedda, our philosophy is simple: deliver the highest quality soap at the best possible value. While beef tallow is naturally rich in vitamins (A, D, E, and K), these vitamins do not survive saponification—the chemical reaction that turns fats into soap.

Instead, it is the beneficial fatty acids found in the tallow that survive the saponification process. In fact, studies show that many of the original fatty acids beef in tallow—including stearic (C18:0), oleic (C18:1n9), linoleic (C18:2n6), and alpha-linolenic (C18:3n3)—remain as unsaponified fatty acids in cold-process soap. These unsaponified oils contribute to a more moisturizing and skin-friendly bar.

For this reason, paying a premium for grass-fed tallow would not improve the performance, feel, or nourishment of the final bar. Some brands highlight "vitamin-rich tallow" for label appeal, but functionally, the finished soap offers the same benefits whether the beef tallow was grass-fed or not.

Evidence:
The retention of fatty acids after saponification – PMC6225244

Ingredient Science FAQs

Why use beef tallow in soap instead of plant oils?

Beef tallow has a fatty acid profile remarkably similar to human sebum, containing oleic, stearic, and palmitic acids that reinforce the skin barrier. Unlike many plant oils, tallow creates a hard, long-lasting bar with creamy lather that doesn't strip natural oils—ideal for sensitive or eczema-prone skin. Research source.

Do vitamins in tallow survive the soap-making process?

Vitamins A, D, E, and K do not survive saponification (the chemical reaction that makes soap). However, the beneficial unsaponified fatty acids—stearic, oleic, linoleic—do remain in the finished bar, providing moisturizing and barrier-supporting benefits. That's why we prioritize fatty acid quality over grass-fed claims for soap. Research source.

Is Bedda soap safe for eczema or rosacea?

Many customers with eczema, rosacea, and allergen-sensitive skin use our Plain Jane (unscented) formula successfully. Tallow's skin-identical lipids help repair the moisture barrier without common irritants like sulfates, synthetic fragrances, or preservatives. Always patch-test first and consult your dermatologist for medical advice.
